My name is Javi Aparicio. I am a portrait photographer working with professionals, entrepreneurs and personal brands in Bern, Switzerland.

From my studio in Bern I make photographs that help people present themselves clearly, professionally and authentically in their communication.

## What kind of photography I do

My focus is portraits. I mainly create:

- Professional portraits
- Photography for LinkedIn and websites
- Personal branding
- Portraits for teams and small businesses

The aim is a strong presence for online profiles and professional communication, for individuals and for teams.

From time to time I also photograph events and other projects. You can find a small selection under [Events]({{events}}).

## How I work

Many people who come to the studio are not used to being photographed. So I keep the session calm and clear: time to settle, concrete direction on posture and expression, without stress.

The aim is that you recognise yourself in the portrait and are glad to use the photographs in your professional communication.

## Where I work

Most sessions take place in my studio, with calm light and no distractions. When the place fits the brief better, I also come to you: office, practice or a spot in Bern and the surrounding area.

## Why I do this work

I believe a good portrait can change how a person shows themselves to the world.

A clear, professional and honest image helps build trust. That matters today for online profiles, websites and social media.
